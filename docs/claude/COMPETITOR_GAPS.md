## Tarnoo differentiators (verified, ours)

- **Free and no account** to plan and export GPX.
- **All of Europe** from one planner (OSM).
- **Elevation + categorised climbs + paved/unpaved surface** on every route.
- **GPX import + export + turn-by-turn**, round-trip generator, multi-day stage
  split with per-stage GPX.
